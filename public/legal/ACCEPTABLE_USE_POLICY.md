# Acceptable Use Policy

**Status: draft for internal and legal review. Not yet approved for reliance by users. This document should be reviewed alongside the [Terms of Service](/legal/TERMS_OF_SERVICE.md) and the [Privacy Policy](/legal/PRIVACY_POLICY.md), which it forms part of.**

Last updated: [date]

## 1. Overview

Klyro is built for legitimate security assessment and vendor risk evaluation: assessing a domain you own or operate, or a supplier you are evaluating with a proper basis to do so. This policy sets out what that does not include.

## 2. Prohibited Uses

### 2a. Illegal activity

Do not use Klyro to facilitate an unlawful act — including hacking, extortion, fraud, or harassment — or to violate a law or regulation applicable to you.

### 2b. Unauthorised assessment

Do not submit a domain you do not own, operate, or have the domain owner's consent to assess. Do not submit an address belonging to an internal or private network; Klyro is built to assess public domains only, and its own network guard refuses to connect to reserved address ranges regardless of what a DNS record claims. Do not use Klyro to search for a vulnerability with the intention of exploiting it — Klyro is reconnaissance, not exploitation, and using it as a first step toward an attack is itself a violation of this policy independent of what happens next.

### 2c. Competitive espionage and targeting

Do not use Klyro to assess a competitor's infrastructure as an act of competitive intelligence gathering, without their consent. Do not use a Klyro finding — a named administrative path, an email authentication gap, a technology identified on the target's site — to plan or inform a social-engineering attempt, a phishing campaign, or any other targeted attack against the assessed organisation or its staff.

### 2d. Circumventing limits

Do not attempt to exceed the Service's rate limits using multiple IP addresses, proxies, or automated rotation. Do not scrape or bulk-download reports or the published methodology beyond ordinary interactive use. Do not attempt to reverse-engineer the scoring methodology or the checks themselves for the purpose of evading detection by them.

### 2e. Misrepresenting findings

Do not alter a Klyro finding's substance when sharing it with a third party, or present it without the context (observed / interpretation / risk / recommendation / evidence / limitation) it was generated with. Do not present Klyro's output as your own independent research, or as the output of a different tool. Do not present a finding, a score, or a clean report as a warranty, an endorsement, or a guarantee of security — a finding states what was observed and what it reasonably indicates; it does not promise an outcome.

## 3. Enforcement

We may suspend or terminate access for a user or organisation that violates this policy, with notice where feasible and immediately where a violation poses an active risk to a third party or to the Service. Repeated violations may result in permanent closure of an account or organisation. Where we reasonably believe activity is unlawful, we may report it to the relevant authorities, and may be required by law to do so.

## 4. Disclaimer

A Klyro finding is informational. It is not security consulting, a warranty, or legal advice, and using the Service does not create a consulting relationship. A clean report does not mean a domain is secure — it means the checks Klyro ran found nothing to report, out of what those checks are able to see, which is stated plainly wherever coverage is incomplete. A concerning finding does not by itself confirm a vulnerability — several findings explicitly limit what they establish, and a finding sourced from a third party (the Network Exposure check) says so and is never presented at the same confidence as a direct observation. You are responsible for interpreting a finding in its full context and for deciding what, if anything, to do about it.
